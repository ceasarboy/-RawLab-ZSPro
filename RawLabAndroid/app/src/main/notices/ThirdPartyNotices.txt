RawLab Android uses the repository's shared Sony2Fuji C++ core.

LibRaw 0.21.5: https://github.com/LibRaw/LibRaw/tree/0.21.5
Source archive: https://github.com/LibRaw/LibRaw/archive/refs/tags/0.21.5.tar.gz
SHA-256: 4b7f183a68f6e46e579e80ba32ab121687e75bd30a2e5566f34c36a6bcba1679
Built from unmodified source as libraw.so; Android CMake build configuration
is supplied in app/src/main/cpp/CMakeLists.txt. LGPL/CDDL license choices and
copyright notices are included in this directory. Optional LCMS, JPEG/JasPer,
RawSpeed and Adobe DNG SDK integrations are not compiled into LibRaw here.

The core's temperature/xy calculation includes Adobe DNG SDK-derived code;
its notice is provided as Adobe-DNG-SDK-LICENSE.txt. Embedded JPEG reading and
JPEG output use bundled stb headers; their notices are included separately.
zlib is provided by the Android system, not bundled as a separate library.
libc++_shared.so is supplied by Android NDK 27.2.12479018; its license is
included as NDK-libcxx-NOTICE in the generated APK assets.

Film LUTs and generated film-label artwork are reused unchanged from this
repository. The artwork is not an official product image or brand endorsement.
See RawLabMac/Resources/FilmIcons/generated-set-a.md and generated-set-b.md
for artwork provenance. Film output follows lutools/docs/color-contract.md.
